What happens when you print?

The browser opens your device’s print dialog. Choose HP DeskJet 2900 series [C2BC32] there if it is not already selected, adjust options, and confirm. The page cannot bypass that confirmation or send jobs directly to the printer.

If the printer is missing from the print dialog, make sure Wi‑Fi Direct is connected and that your device supports printing to the printer. Some devices may need the HP Smart app, AirPrint, Mopria, or a printer driver.
